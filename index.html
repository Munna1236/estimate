About Type 2 Buildings

Type 2 buildings are reinforced concrete (RCC) framed structures, one of the most common construction types in India. These buildings feature a structural skeleton of concrete beams and columns with infill walls, offering excellent strength, durability, and flexibility in design.

Key Characteristics:

  • Structure: Reinforced concrete frame with brick or block masonry infill walls
  • Load Bearing: Primarily frame-based, allowing for large column-free spaces
  • Fire Resistance: Excellent fire resistance due to concrete construction
  • Common Use: Residential complexes, office buildings, commercial spaces, and educational institutions
  • Durability: 50-100 years with proper maintenance
  • Cost Efficiency: Moderate construction cost with good quality assurance

Cost Estimation Factors:

  • Quality Grades: Standard (basic finishes), Premium (enhanced finishes), Luxury (high-end materials and finishes)
  • Location Impact: Urban centers have higher labor and material costs compared to tier-2 or tier-3 cities
  • Floor Count: Multi-story buildings benefit from economies of scale, reducing per-unit costs
  • Material Adjustment: Market fluctuations in steel, cement, and other materials affect final costs

Note: This estimator provides approximate costs based on industry averages. Actual costs may vary based on site conditions, design complexity, and local market conditions. Consult with professionals for accurate project budgeting.
